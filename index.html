PLAYER UNKNOWN BATTLEGROUNDS

PUBG is a battle royale that pits 100 players against each other. Players will land, loot, and survive in a shrinking battleground as they outplay their opponents to become the lone survivor.

Game Features

Matches start with players parachuting from a transport plane into massive, varied open-world maps.

Competitive seasons where players can grind through tiers to earn exclusive rewards, badges, and weapon skins.

Features true-to-life bullet physics, including bullet drop and travel time, which requires you to actively lead shots.

Players start with nothing. You must quickly loot weapons, scopes, tactical gear, and medical supplies from abandoned buildings and drop zones.

WINNER WINNER CHICKEN DINNER

PUBG is a multiplayer battle royale game where 100 players parachute onto an island with no gear and must scavenge buildings for weapons, armor, and supplies to survive. As the match progresses, a deadly electric storm continually shrinks the playable map, forcing the remaining players into closer, high-stakes combat. Players can compete alone or in small teams, utilizing vehicles and tactical cover to eliminate rivals and avoid detection. The ultimate goal is to outlast everyone else, as the last surviving person or squad wins the match and earns the legendary title, "Winner Winner Chicken Dinner!"
